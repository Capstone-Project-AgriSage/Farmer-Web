## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Philosophy
The design system embodies modern precision agriculture, technological reliability, and down-to-earth utilitarian clarity. Designed to serve a dual audience—store managers/warehouse staff executing high-velocity transactions, and local farmers seeking transparent inventory, credit, and computer-vision crop diagnostics—the system prioritizes unshakeable trust, legibility under varied working conditions, and pragmatic simplicity.

### Visual Style: Modern Agricultural SaaS
The aesthetic is clean, functional Corporate / Modern with tactile micro-cues:
- **Clean Surfaces:** Pristine white and faint vegetative-tinted surfaces balance dense data grids and analytical workflows.
- **Dignified Green Primary:** Natural forest chlorophyll green (#2E7D32) commands authority without synthetic hyper-saturation, reassuring users with institutional solidity.
- **Pragmatic Restraint:** Deliberately avoids high-tech cyberpunk gradients, deep dark-mode heavy skins, or decorative glassmorphism. It conveys the poise of a mission-critical ERP combined with the ease of consumer mobile-first commerce.
- **Advisory AI Distinction:** Crop disease diagnostic readouts feel analytical, scientific, and clearly assistive rather than authoritarian or speculative.

## Colors

### Color Strategy
The system adopts an agricultural palette built on verified high-contrast tokens. Primary green (`#2E7D32`) is the principal action color for confirmation, critical interactive buttons, active tab indicators, and verified safe statuses.

### Surface Architecture
- **Base Canvas (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity white across standard cards, form inputs, and content panels.
- **Muted Background (`#F7FAF7`):** Cool, slightly desaturated sage off-white for full app scaffolds, eliminating blinding eye-strain during extended field and POS work.
- **Secondary Surfaces (`#EDF4EE`):** Structural borders, table headers, and inactive navigational rail elements.

### Semantic Domain Mapping
- **Inventory & Operations:**
  - `In Stock` / `Paid` / `Completed` / `Received`: `status-success` (`#2E7D32`) on `status-success-surface` (`#E8F5E9`).
  - `Low Stock` / `Pending` / `Partial` / `Ordered`: `status-warning` (`#E65100`) on `status-warning-surface` (`#FFF3E0`).
  - `Out of Stock` / `Cancelled` / `Overdue Debt`: `status-error` (`#C62828`) on `status-error-surface` (`#FFEBEE`).
  - `Processing` / `Draft` / `Model Diagnostic Evaluation`: `status-info` (`#0277BD`) on `status-info-surface` (`#E1F5FE`).
- **AI Diagnostics & Confidence Tiering:**
  - $\ge 85\%$ Confidence: High reassurance (`#2E7D32`).
  - $60\% - 84\%$ Confidence: Moderate advisory note (`#EF6C00`).
  - $< 60\%$ Confidence: Mandatory warning banner requiring human agronomist review (`#C62828`).

## Typography

### Hierarchy and Usability
The system relies entirely on Inter to ensure clean rendering across desktop browsers, rugged Android tablets, and farmer smartphones.
- **Display & Headlines:** Reserved for primary metric dashboards, AI diagnostic overview headers, and customer store banners. Strict negative tracking balances the wide glyphs of Inter at higher scales.
- **Body Text:** Scaled comfortably with an open `line-height` (`22px` on `14px` body) to guarantee legibility when reading detailed crop treatment instructions, active pesticide ingredient concentrations, and multi-line invoice items.
- **Monospaced Identifiers:** SKU codes, batch lot serials, and AI Model identifiers (`model: crop-v3.2-fp16`) utilize standard tabular alignment (`tnum` font feature) to simplify invoice verification and ledger alignment.

## Layout & Spacing

### Grid & Layout Structure
- **B2B / SaaS Operation Hub (Admin, Manager, Staff):**
  - Rigid fixed-sidebar + fluid content model.
  - Sidebar: fixed width `260px` (`68px` collapsed on tablets), containing role-filtered navigation items.
  - Top header: fixed height `64px` with quick global search, active notification drawer, and user profile switcher.
  - Main workspace: responsive fluid canvas with a max content width container bounded at `1600px` for high-density data tables and ERP ledger reconciliation.
- **Farmer / Storefront (Customer Layout):**
  - Max container width of `1280px` centered with fluid margins.
  - Mobile-first approach: single-column full-width stacking transitioning to 12-column CSS Grid at desktop breakpoint (`1024px`).

### Breakpoints
- **Mobile (`< 640px`):** Single column. Data tables convert to card listings. Sidebar folds into an off-canvas drawer. Tap targets enforce a minimum of 44x44px.
- **Tablet (`640px - 1023px`):** 6-column grid. Collapsible navigation bar. Split views for POS cashiering and invoice review.
- **Desktop (`>= 1024px`):** 12-column grid. Permanent sidebar. Multi-pane panels for AI crop image diagnosis (image alongside diagnosis & treatment recommendations).

## Elevation & Depth

### Depth Paradigm
Depth is communicated through functional tonal separation paired with soft ambient shadows. Heavy 3D skeuomorphism and glossy glassmorphism are forbidden to preserve clarity and daylight field contrast.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat / Base):** Used for standard data backgrounds (`#FFFFFF` or `#F7FAF7`) with crisp perimeter division using `border-subtle` (`#E1E8E2`).
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks):** 
  - `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(23, 33, 24, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(23, 33, 24, 0.05)`
  - Encased with a 1px border (`#E1E8E2`). Used for stats cards, inventory rows, and product cards.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Actionable Elements):**
  - `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(23, 33, 24, 0.07), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(23, 33, 24, 0.05)`
  - Translates up by `1px` on hover to signal clickability.
- **Level 3 (Dropdowns, Flyouts & Tooltips):**
  - `box-shadow: 0 10px 15px -3px rgba(23, 33, 24, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(23, 33, 24, 0.04)`
- **Level 4 (Modals, Takeover Drawers & QR Cashier Overlays):**
  - `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(23, 33, 24, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(23, 33, 24, 0.06)`
  - Paired with an accessible backdrop scrim: `rgba(23, 33, 24, 0.45)`.

## Shapes

### Corner Curvature Strategy
The interface utilizes an intermediate roundedness scale (`roundedness: 2`, where base radius is `0.5rem` / `8px`):
- **Base Elements (`rounded-md`, 6px - 8px):** Buttons, inputs, search bars, selects, and table action triggers. Strikes a balance between business-grade structure and friendly accessibility.
- **Containers & Surfaces (`rounded-lg` / `rounded-xl`, 12px - 16px):** KPI stat widgets, diagnostic preview cards, modals, and QR payment dialogs.
- **Status Pills & Chips (`rounded-full`, 9999px):** Status badges (e.g., `PAID`, `LOW STOCK`, `PENDING`), AI probability tags, and numeric counter badges.

## Components

### 1. Buttons & Triggers
- **Primary Action:** Solid background `primary-color-hex` (`#2E7D32`), text white, `rounded-md`, font weight 600. Hover: `primary-hover` (`#256628`). Active: `primary-active` (`#1E5220`). Focus: 2px offset ring with `#2E7D32`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background transparent, border 1.5px solid `border-strong` (`#B0BEB3`), text `text-primary`. Hover: background `surface-subtle` (`#F7FAF7`) with border `#2E7D32`.
- **Danger / Destructive:** Background `status-error-surface`, text `status-error`, border 1px solid `status-error`. Hover: solid background `status-error`, text white.
- **Size Options:**
  - Compact (Table actions): Height `32px`, text `13px`, padding `0 12px`.
  - Standard (Forms, POS): Height `40px`, text `14px`, padding `0 16px`.
  - Large (Checkout / Mobile Buy Now): Height `48px`, text `16px`, padding `0 24px`.

### 2. Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Text Inputs & Search:** Background white, border 1px solid `border-subtle` (`#E1E8E2`), height `40px`, inner padding `0 12px`. Hover: border `border-strong`. Focus: border `primary-color-hex`, box-shadow `0 0 0 3px rgba(46, 125, 50, 0.15)`.
- **Error State:** Border `status-error`, focus ring red tint, helper text in `status-error`.
- **Quantity Adjuster:** Stepper layout with unified bordered pill container; central tabular number display flanked by `-` and `+` touch-friendly icon buttons.

### 3. Data Tables
- **Header:** Background `surface-secondary` (`#EDF4EE`), text `text-secondary`, font size `12px`, uppercase, medium weight with sort arrow indicators.
- **Rows:** Alternating subtle hover states (`#F7FAF7`). Row height `52px` for comfortable touch and scanner interaction. Bottom cell divider 1px `border-subtle`.
- **Actions Cell:** Right-aligned inline icon-buttons for View, Edit, and conditional context menu.

### 4. Status Badges & Pills
- Rendered with `rounded-full`, padding `4px 10px`, font size `11px`, bold, uppercase:
  - **Orders:** `CONFIRMED` (green surface/green text), `DRAFT` (gray surface/gray text), `CANCELLED` (red surface/red text).
  - **Inventory:** `IN STOCK` (green), `LOW STOCK` (orange/warning), `OUT OF STOCK` (red/error).
  - **Payments & Debt:** `PAID` (green), `PARTIAL` (amber), `UNPAID` (red).

### 5. Cards
- **Product Card:** Image frame (1:1 aspect ratio with subtle background `#F7FAF7`), Category tag, Product Name (`title-sm`), Active Ingredient caption (`body-sm`, `text-muted`), Unit Price in prominent green, stock availability badge, and quick "+ Cart" button.
- **Stat / KPI Card:** White background, 1px border, top-tier icon container in `surface-subtle`, prominent metric value (`headline-md`), and comparison delta text indicating weekly/monthly change.

### 6. AI Diagnosis Component Suite
- **Image Upload Zone:** Drag-and-drop zone with dashed border (`border-strong`), sample thumbnail preview, crop-type selector dropdown, and symptom observation textarea.
- **Diagnosis Result Card:**
  - Header: Uploaded crop image paired with identified disease title.
  - **Confidence Indicator:** Progress bar visualizer filled with dynamic semantic coloring (Green: $\ge 85\%$, Orange: $60-84\%$, Red: $< 60\%$).
  - **Alternative Candidates (Top-k):** Collapsible accordion displaying secondary predictions with their percentage distributions.
  - **Disclaimer Callout:** Persistent advisory callout box with `status-info` or `status-warning` styling stating AI advisory limits and agronomic verification notices.
  - **Recommended Product List:** Horizontal carousel or stacked mini-cards of remedial fungicides/fertilizers directly actionable with "Add to Cart" or "Prescribe to Farmer".

### 7. Customer Debt & Financial Widget
- **Debt Gauge:** Visual progress bar contrasting `Current Debt` against authorized `Credit Limit`.
- **Threshold Alert:** Triggers orange notification at $80\%$ usage, and saturated red lockdown alert once credit limit is exhausted or past-due terms are reached.

### 8. Cash & QR Payment Modal
- Clean modal with segmented toggle (`Cash` vs `VietQR / Bank Transfer`).
- QR View: Centered high-contrast QR code container (`220x220px`), transfer memo, dynamic transaction countdown timer, and live status badge (`Waiting for scan...` switching immediately to `Payment Confirmed`).
